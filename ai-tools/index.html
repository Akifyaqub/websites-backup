Head to head

ChatGPT vs Claude vs Perplexity

The three general tools almost everyone in the US starts with. All three have a genuinely usable free tier — the paid plans differ in what they're best at, not in quality.

ChatGPT

Free · Plus $20/mo

Best at
Doing a bit of everything: drafting, images, voice, data, custom GPTs for repeat tasks like listing descriptions or parent emails.
Watch out
Answers don't cite sources by default — check facts before you send anything to a client or a classroom.
Pick it if
You want one subscription that covers the most jobs.

Claude

Free · Pro $20/mo

Best at
Long, careful writing and big documents — contracts, reports, manuscripts. Its drafts usually need the least editing.
Watch out
Fewer extras than ChatGPT: no image generation of its own and a smaller app ecosystem.
Pick it if
Writing quality matters more than features.

Perplexity

Free · Pro $20/mo · Students $10/mo

Best at
Research with receipts: every answer links its sources, so students and professionals can verify claims in one click.
Watch out
It's a research engine, not a drafting partner — long creative writing is not its strength.
Pick it if
You need answers you can cite — homework, market checks, fact-finding.

Before you pay

Pick the right tool in five minutes

1

Name the one job

“Write my listing descriptions” beats “help with marketing”. One job means one tool, not five subscriptions.

2

Start on the free plan

12 of the 15 tools here have one. Run your real work through it for a week before paying anything.

3

Check the privacy fit

Student data needs FERPA/COPPA tools; patient talk needs HIPAA. The profession-built tools above already carry those badges.

4

Pay monthly first

Yearly billing is cheaper but locks you in. Go monthly for the first month — switch once the tool has earned it.

Questions

Asked by US readers

What is the best free AI tool for teachers?

Start with MagicSchool AI — its free plan covers lesson plans, rubrics, quizzes and parent emails, and it's built for K–12 privacy rules (FERPA and COPPA). If your students will use the AI directly, Khanmigo is free for US teachers and tutors by guiding instead of handing over answers.

Is ChatGPT free in the USA?

Yes. The free tier includes everyday chat, limited uploads, image creation and deep research. Plus ($20/month) raises the limits and adds advanced reasoning models, projects and custom GPTs. Prices checked on OpenAI's pricing page, November 22, 2026.

Which AI tool should a small business start with?

A general assistant first — ChatGPT or Claude, both free to start — for emails, quotes, social posts and paperwork. Add one specialist only when a job repeats: Fathom or Otter for meeting notes, Jasper if marketing copy is a weekly task.

Are these tools safe for student or patient information?

The profession-built ones are designed for it: MagicSchool and Brisk cite FERPA/COPPA compliance, and Freed is HIPAA-compliant for clinical notes. General chatbots are not covered the same way — keep student names and patient details out of them, and follow your school district's or clinic's policy.

How were these 15 tools chosen?

Three rules: the tool must do one profession's job noticeably better than a general chatbot, it must publish US pricing (or a free plan), and its price and free tier were verified on the vendor's own site in November 2026. Tools that failed any rule were left out.
